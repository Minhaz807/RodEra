RodEraBangladesh Solar One-Stop
Planning-stage tool. Every price is a market assumption carried from the source workbook — get live quotes before committing capital.
STEP 1 OF 9

Project setup

Tell the platform who you are, what you are building and where. Everything downstream — sizing rules, equipment defaults, tariff treatment and approvals — keys off these answers.

STEP 2 OF 9

Electrical load calculation

Build the connected load from real equipment. Enter ratings in W, kW, HP or kVA — the platform converts, applies power factor, efficiency, diversity and operating pattern, then derives maximum demand and an hourly profile.

STEP 3 OF 9

Roof & area analysis

Total area steps down to usable area, PV array area, walkways, equipment and safety setbacks — and out the other end, the maximum capacity the site carries. It runs in reverse too: name a capacity and get the area it needs.

STEP 4 OF 9

Solar resource

Peak sun hours and irradiation for the selected location, month by month, with the conservative design value the rest of the model uses.

STEP 5 OF 9

System design

Module count, string configuration, DC/AC ratio, inverter and battery sizing, cables, protection, earthing and the rest of the balance of system. Every auto-calculated value can be overridden.

STEP 6 OF 9

Equipment selection

Searchable Bangladesh-market catalogue carried from the source workbook. Prices, suppliers and specifications are reproduced as recorded there — nothing is generated. Each row carries its own source note and date.

STEP 7 OF 9

Generation & energy balance

Monthly and annual yield from the loss stack, then hour-by-hour generation set against the load profile to split self-consumption, export, import and battery movement.

STEP 8 OF 9

CAPEX & OPEX

Bill of quantities priced from your equipment selections, then the delivery route — self-install or EPC turnkey — and the twenty-year lifecycle cost including replacements.

STEP 9 OF 9

Financial model

Year-by-year cash flow on the energy actually monetised — self-consumption at retail tariff, export at the buyback rate — with debt service, NPV, IRR, payback, LCOE, ROI and DSCR.

Dashboard

One page for the whole project. Every figure is live — change anything upstream and this updates.

Scenarios

Save the current state as a named scenario, change assumptions, save again, and compare side by side.

Solar knowledge base

Full form, plain definition, the engineering behind it, the formula, a worked example and where it applies. Technical terms are clickable throughout the platform.

Bangladesh renewable energy

Institutions, the policy and circular library carried from the workbook, the SREDA type-tested module register, and the supplier names on record.

References & audit trail

Every assumption in the model with its value, where it came from and what it drives. Change any of them here.

Excel export

Generates a multi-sheet workbook of the whole project. Live formulas are written into the calculation sheets so the workbook stays editable after export.

Platform identity

The naming shortlist, the selected working name, and the visual system used across this build.
